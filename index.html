⚡ Companion Browser Extension (1-Click Watchlist Sync)

Because Netflix, Prime Video, and Apple TV+ block third-party websites from reading your account directly, use our included Streaming Watchlists Sync Chrome/Brave/Edge extension in streaming-watchlists/extension:

  1. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome, Brave, or Edge and toggle on Developer mode (top-right).
  2. Click Load unpacked and select the folder /Users/duncanmitchell/Downloads/streaming-watchlists/extension (also enable "Allow access to file URLs" on the extension details page).
  3. Click any service below to log in & open your Watchlist, then click the floating 🔄 Sync Watchlist button on that page!
